(02) — O meniAbout

Web developer sa obrazovanjem iz informatike, fokusiran na moderne sajtove i praktične web aplikacije. I'm a web developer with a background in Informatics, focused on building modern websites and practical web applications.

Volim da radim kroz ceo proces razvoja — od planiranja i dizajna interfejsa do implementacije i objavljivanja. Stalno unapređujem svoje veštine, istražujem nove tehnologije i pravim projekte koji rešavaju stvarne probleme. Zanimaju me prilike u kojima mogu da doprinesem, učim od iskusnijih developera i rastem kao softverski inženjer. I enjoy working across the entire development process — from planning and interface design to implementation and deployment. I'm continuously improving my skills, exploring new technologies, and building projects that solve real-world problems. I'm interested in opportunities where I can contribute, learn from experienced developers, and grow as a software engineer.
